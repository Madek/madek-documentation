# Ops notes

Thin home for practical operational how-tos that belong in this technical
docs repo (not a full deploy/hosting handbook). Slack is a source of
operational knowledge, not the place to maintain it — extract useful
procedures here over time.

Examples of content that fits:

- Blocking abusive IPs (e.g. after a bot attack) via Apache / reverse proxy
- Other short runbooks that developers or system-minded admins need

Concrete runbooks are **deferred** until extracted from team practice. Deploy
and hosting remain in the `deploy` submodule / ops tooling — not duplicated
here as a full handbook.
